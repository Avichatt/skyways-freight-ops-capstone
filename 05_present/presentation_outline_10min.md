# Ten-minute presentation outline

Eight slides. Timings are hard limits; rehearse with a timer three times.

| # | Slide | Time | What is on it | What you say |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The pain | 1:00 | One scene: the rainy evening, four trucks, two docks, the coordinator on the phone. One number (minutes lost or detention cost) | The problem as the planner feels it, with the number |
| 2 | The root cause | 1:00 | Your Five Whys as a vertical chain, root cause highlighted | Read the chain upward as "therefore" |
| 3 | What I built, in one picture | 1:00 | C4 container diagram of your system | Name each box and one sentence on what it owns |
| 4 | The decision that mattered | 1:00 | Your most important ADR as a three-option table | Why you chose, what it cost |
| 5 | Live demo | 3:00 | The running system | Follow `demo_script.md` exactly |
| 6 | Proof | 1:00 | The test output or the metric table | One claim, one number, one file name |
| 7 | What it cannot do yet | 0:30 | Three limits, honestly stated | Where the enterprise version differs |
| 8 | What I would do next | 0:30 | Two or three improvements with effort estimates | Close on the one you would start tomorrow |

Rules
- No slide has more than 30 words outside the diagram.
- The demo slide is a backup screenshot in case the live run fails. Say "here is what you would have seen" and keep moving.
- Every number on a slide exists in a file in the repository.
