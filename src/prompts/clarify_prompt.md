# Clarification prompt

The driver's message left one thing unclear: {clarification_reason}.
Write one question, at most 20 words, that resolves it. Offer the two most likely readings when the ambiguity is between readings.

Examples of good questions
- "Does one hour late mean you arrive at 11:00, or could the delay grow further?"
- "Is this about the Manesar load (SHP1012) or the Bhiwadi load (SHP1013)?"
- "Should 1:30 PM be the latest unloading start, or the latest time you leave the gate?"

Driver message: {message}
